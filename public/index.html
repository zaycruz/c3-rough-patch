Challenge 3 · Rough weeks

A week's warning before a rough patch

Every evening it reads a survivor's own check-ins, their wearable and their treatment calendar, and forecasts whether a rough patch starts in the next 7 days. A rough patch is two bad days within 3 days of each other, or an ER visit, admission or treatment pause.

What it means for a coach

About one new alert per coach per day on a 60-person caseload, with a median 6 days of lead. Enough time to send a short message or move the call earlier.

What to do

Treat an alert as a nudge to reach out, not a diagnosis. About 1 alert in 5.5 is followed by a rough patch within a week.

Urgent tier

Call today: ER-level risk

A separate model flags the top 3% of days for an ER visit or admission. It catches 47% of them 2 to 7 days early, against 36% from the calendar. A flag carries about 6 times the usual risk (7.4% vs 1.2%): a reason to call, not a diagnosis.

Know the limits

  • Lead time is shown on synthetic data only. The patterns in it were designed in.
  • 55% of daily check-ins are missing. A run of skipped check-ins is itself a warning sign (1.8 times after 5 or more).
  • Sarcoma recall is clearly lower (13%). Several groups are too small to judge.

Endpoints

JSON over HTTPS, no key needed. Contract in CONTRACT.md.

  • GET/api/c3/survivors→Tonight's caseload: 60 survivors in priority order, urgent first, each with a tier and a one-line reason.
  • GET/api/c3/survivors/:id→One survivor: 7-day risk, the signals behind it, charts against their own baseline and a suggested action.
  • GET/api/c3/metrics→Held-out results for every method, calibration, fairness by group, the real-data check and a ready-made headline.

Reproduce

Fixed seed and a split by patient, from the raw download to the served JSON.

github.com/zaycruz/c3-rough-patch →
  1. npm install

    Set up the Python venv first (README › Run it). The data is read from the Challenge 3 download.

  2. npm run build:data

    Labels, features, models and the held-out evaluation. About 3 minutes.

  3. npm run e2e

    Checks every live endpoint; saves a JSON record under runs/.
